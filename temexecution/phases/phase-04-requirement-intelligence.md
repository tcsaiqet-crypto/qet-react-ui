# Phase 04 - Requirement Intelligence

## Objective
Turn validated understanding into categorized, traceable requirements.

## Steps
1. Check categorization feature configuration.
2. Validate understanding requirements exist.
3. Categorize requirements and preserve source evidence.
4. Validate taxonomy and coverage mappings.
5. Persist category artifacts and provenance.

## Exit Gate
Categorization is explicitly completed or explicitly skipped by configuration; no fake completed state is emitted.
