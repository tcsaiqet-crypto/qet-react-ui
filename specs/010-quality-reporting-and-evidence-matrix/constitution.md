# Constitution 010: Quality Reporting Non-Negotiables

1. **Sign-off Objectivity**: Executive sign-off recommendations (GO / NO_GO / CONDITIONAL_GO) must be computed strictly based on pass rates and defect severity rules.
2. **Deterministic Artifacts**: HTML and PDF reports must be generated and persisted in `uploads/{run_id}/artifacts/reports/` for audit compliance.
3. **Traceability**: Every report must include requirement-to-test execution traceability matrices.
