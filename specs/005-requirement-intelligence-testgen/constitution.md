# Constitution 005: Requirement Intelligence Non-Negotiables

1. **Taxonomy Integrity**: Requirements must be classified into the standard 10 categories (Functional, Business Rules, Data Validation, Security, Compliance, Integration, Accessibility, Performance, Edge Case, UI/UX).
2. **Coverage Traceability**: Every requirement must link directly to generated test cases.
3. **No Uncategorized Requirements**: Requirements failing categorization must be flagged explicitly as `Uncategorized` rather than silently dropped.
